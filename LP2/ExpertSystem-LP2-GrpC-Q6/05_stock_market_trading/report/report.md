# Stock Market Trading Expert System Report

## Objective

To build a simple expert system that suggests buy, sell, or hold.

## Inputs

- Price trend
- Company news
- Risk level
- Trading volume

## Knowledge Base

The knowledge base contains simple trading signal rules.

## Sample Rules

- If trend is up, news is positive, and risk is not high, suggest buy.
- If trend is down or news is negative, suggest sell or avoid.
- If risk is high or volume is low, suggest hold.
- Otherwise, suggest hold and monitor.

## Conclusion

The system gives a basic trading suggestion using rule-based reasoning. It is only for academic demonstration.
